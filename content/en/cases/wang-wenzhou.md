## Planning Journey

1. **Planning began in the first semester of grade 11**
   - A student from a regular Chinese high school with no tradition of sending students abroad
   - Little time and a lot to do, so precise positioning was essential

2. **Summer school strategy**
   - Planned and supported applications to American-style university summer schools in China
   - Helped obtain a professor's recommendation letter to make up for the school's limited track record

3. **Activity design**
   - Helped find a nonprofit organization to work with
   - Connected the activity to the student's strengths, creating a distinctive application profile

4. **Development after admission**
   - Advised on choosing a major, and the student settled on computer science
   - Supported the master's application, leading to admission to Columbia University's master's program in finance
