## Graduate Admissions Service

Unlike undergraduate applications, our graduate admissions service places a strong focus on each student's future career path.

### What's Included

1. **Academic Direction Assessment**
   - Matching the undergraduate major with a graduate field
   - Assessing the feasibility of switching fields
   - Choosing between academic and professional programs

2. **School Assessment**
   - Weighing overall rankings against program rankings
   - Location and career resources
   - Faculty and research areas

3. **Career Path Planning**
   - Industry trend analysis
   - Connections to internship and job opportunities
   - Building an alumni network

4. **Application Documents**
   - Personal statement (PS)
   - Research proposal
   - Writing sample
   - Recommendation letter strategy

5. **Interview Coaching**
   - Mock interviews
   - Technical interview preparation
   - Behavioral interview skills

### Why Fox Society

- Extensive career guidance resources in North America and China
- A strong professional network
- We don't just raise admission chances; we help students plan their careers ahead of time
