## Year-Round Planning Service

Our year-round plan covers the student's positioning and development, a detailed monthly schedule, summer program recommendations and application support, research and academic resources, and standardized test scheduling.

### What's Included

1. **Student Positioning and Development**
   - Personality and interest assessments
   - Academic ability assessment
   - Competitive strengths analysis
   - Building a personal brand

2. **Detailed Monthly Plan**
   - Monthly goal setting
   - Breaking down and tracking tasks
   - Reviewing progress and adjusting the plan
   - Regular communication with parents

3. **Summer Program Recommendations**
   - Summer school application guidance
   - Matching research projects
   - Internship recommendations
   - Competition planning

4. **Research and Academic Resources**
   - Connections with professors
   - Laboratory resources
   - Guidance on publishing papers
   - Academic conference recommendations

5. **Standardized Test Planning**
   - SAT/ACT scheduling
   - TOEFL/IELTS preparation strategy
   - AP/IB course planning
   - Test registration and score reporting

### Why Fox Society

- Thorough, all-round annual planning
- Helps students manage their time better
- Well-planned activities
- Matching the right resources to improve application efficiency and results
