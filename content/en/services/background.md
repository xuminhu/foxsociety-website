## Profile Building Service

Research professors from top universities in the US and UK guide students through academic research projects in a range of fields, using project-based learning (PBL).

### What's Included

1. **Summer School Applications**
   - Selecting summer schools at top universities
   - Preparing application materials
   - Essay and recommendation letter guidance
   - Visa and pre-departure preparation

2. **Competition Planning**
   - Choosing academic competitions
   - Building a preparation plan
   - Connecting with competition resources
   - Developing teamwork skills

3. **Research Projects**
   - Connections with professors
   - Designing a research topic
   - Paper writing guidance
   - Support for publishing results

4. **Reading and Writing Training**
   - Academic reading methods
   - Developing critical thinking
   - Academic writing conventions
   - Literature review skills

### Project-Based Learning (PBL)

- **Explore interests**: students develop and explore their own fields of interest
- **Train logical thinking**: build critical thinking and analytical skills
- **Improve writing**: academic writing and communication skills
- **Confirm application direction**: become more certain about the field and universities to apply to

### Why It Matters

Summer schools, competitions and research projects all help students understand themselves better on the road to applying: what interests them, where their strengths lie, and which subject they are willing to work hard for. They broaden students' horizons and help them grow on the international stage.
